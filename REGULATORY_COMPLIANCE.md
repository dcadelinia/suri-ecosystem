# Multi-Jurisdictional Environmental & Technical Compliance Manifest

The SuRI system enforces automated compliance boundaries at the edge to adhere to distinct legal requirements in each physical node environment:

### 🇺🇸 State of Texas (Industrial & Utility Nodes)
* **Texas Water Code Compliance:** Filtration backwash discharge is automatically monitored to ensure compliance with the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ) storm water utility benchmarks.
* **OSHA Anti-Static Directives:** Server rack consumables (such as the anti-static green hex-key wrench handles and SFP port dust covers) printed on-site must maintain a surface resistivity under 10^9 ohms to comply with electrical safety standards.

### 🇵🇭 Republic of the Philippines (Coastal & Marine Nodes)
* **Philippine Clean Water Act (RA 9275):** Coastal filtering nodes must verify that any filtered seawater return flows do not disrupt localized nutrient baselines.
* **E-Waste Management Directives:** Scrap raw materials extracted via the e-waste stream must pass automated near-infrared (NIR) purity audits before processing to ensure zero toxic chemical leaching into marine tool outputs.

### 🌍 Sub-Saharan Africa (Agricultural & Infrastructure Nodes)
* **Regional Environmental Protection Standards:** 3D-printed agricultural utilities (such as drip irrigation nozzles and spigots) are restricted to processing Food-Grade Polypropylene (PP) or High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE) flakes, ensuring zero chemical leaching into rural drinking water supplies or agricultural soil matrices.
